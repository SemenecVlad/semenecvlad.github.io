Stripix — Support

Write to vladislavsemenets.ua@gmail.com — we read every message and usually reply within a couple of days.

The quickest way is Settings → Contact support in the app: it opens Mail with the app version, device model and iOS version already filled in. For anything about a purchase, add the date and which plan you bought.

I reinstalled the app or got a new iPhone, and Pro is gone

Open Settings → Restore purchases (or Restore on the Pro screen). Your plan belongs to your Apple Account, so as long as you are signed in with the same one, it comes back. Restoring needs an internet connection once.

It says “Couldn’t verify your subscription”

Pro works offline, but the app needs to confirm your plan with the App Store from time to time. If it stays offline for a long time after the plan’s renewal date, Pro pauses until it can check again. Connect to the internet and tap Check again on the Home screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app: Settings → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Stripix. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date; Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Stripix is missing from the Share Sheet

Open the Share Sheet from Photos or Files, scroll the row of apps to the end and tap More. Find Stripix and turn it on; you can drag it to the front of the list.

How do I run a workflow from Shortcuts?

Save a workflow in the app first. Then, in the Shortcuts app, add an action and search for “Stripix”: your saved workflows appear there and in the Share Sheet.

A file won’t open or convert

Stripix reads what iOS can decode on your device. Most RAW formats work, but support for some cameras depends on the iPhone model and iOS version. If a file fails, send it to us if you can, or tell us the camera and file type.

The app can’t see my photos

Check iOS Settings → Stripix → Photos. “Limited Access” works fine: the app sees only the photos you selected, and you can add more there.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens on your iPhone — try it in Airplane Mode. See the Privacy Policy.
