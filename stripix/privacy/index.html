Stripix — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026

Short version: your photos and files never leave your iPhone. The only thing that goes online is your purchase, checked with an anonymous ID.

This policy explains what information Stripix: Photo Converter for iPhone (“the app”) collects and how it is used. The app is made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”).

1. Your photos and files

Everything the app does to your photos and PDFs — converting, resizing, compressing, reading and removing metadata, watermarks, text recognition (OCR) and making PDFs — runs on your device. Your files are never uploaded, and we never receive them. The app works fully in Airplane Mode.

Metadata such as camera details, dates and location coordinates is read and removed on the device. Coordinates are shown to you as numbers and are never sent anywhere.

While a job runs, the app keeps working copies in its temporary storage and deletes them as soon as the job ends. Results go only where you send them: your photo library, the Files app or the share sheet.

2. Photos access

You pick photos with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you choose. Saving results needs “Add Photos Only” access. The app asks for access to your library only for features that need to read it, and “Limited Access” is fully supported. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Stripix → Photos.

3. Settings and workflows

Your settings and saved workflows are stored on your device, in storage shared only with the app’s own Share Sheet extension and Shortcuts actions. Deleting the app deletes them.

To keep Pro working after a reinstall, the app keeps a copy of your plan status (plan, expiry date, time of the last check) in your device’s Keychain. It stays on the device.

4. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment card details. To confirm and restore your plan, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives:

  • an anonymous identifier created for your installation of the app;
  • your purchase and transaction history for this app;
  • basic technical information needed for the request, such as the app and iOS version, device model, App Store country and IP address.

This information is used only to tell the app which plan you have. It is not linked to your identity, there is no account, and it is not used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat never sees your photos or files. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

5. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not ask to track you across apps and websites, and we have no servers of our own.

6. Crash reports

If you turn on Share With App Developers in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. This is controlled by Apple and by you, not by the app. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

7. Contacting support

“Contact support” in the app opens your own Mail app with a draft that includes the app version, device model and iOS version; nothing is sent automatically, and you can edit the draft before sending it. If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message and use them only to answer you. We delete them on request.

8. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, restrict or object to its processing, and lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. We hold no personal data about you except emails you send us. The purchase records kept by RevenueCat are anonymous; if you want them deleted, write to us with the date of the purchase and the order ID from your Apple receipt, and we will find and delete them.

10. Sharing of information

We do not sell or share your personal information. RevenueCat processes purchase information on our behalf, only as described above.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
