Stripix — Support

Write to support@stripix.app — we read every message and usually reply within a couple of days.

The quickest way is Settings → Contact support in the app: it opens your mail app with the app version, device model and system version already filled in. For anything about a purchase, add the date and which plan you bought.

I reinstalled the app or got a new iPhone, and Pro is gone

Open Settings → Restore purchases (or Restore on the Pro screen). Your plan belongs to your Apple Account, so as long as you are signed in with the same one, it comes back. Restoring needs an internet connection once.

Restore Pro on Android

Sign in to Google Play with the Google account that bought Pro, then open Settings → Restore purchases in the app. Restoring needs an internet connection once.

It says “Couldn’t verify your subscription”

Pro works offline, but the app needs to confirm your plan with the App Store (Google Play on Android) from time to time. If it stays offline for a long time after the plan’s renewal date, Pro pauses until it can check again. Connect to the internet and tap Check again on the Home screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the app: Settings → Manage subscription. Or on your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Stripix. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date; Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for. If you cancel during the free trial, you are not charged.

On Android: Settings → Manage subscription in the app, or the Play Store app → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Stripix. Cancel any time before the renewal date.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store, not by us. App Store purchases: reportaproblem.apple.com. Google Play purchases: Google Play refunds.

Stripix is missing from the Share Sheet

Open the Share Sheet from Photos or Files, scroll the row of apps to the end and tap More. Find Stripix and turn it on; you can drag it to the front of the list. On Android, Stripix’s actions (Convert, Clean metadata, Image → PDF, Compress) appear in the share menu; on some phones a newly installed app first sits behind More.

How do I run a workflow from Shortcuts?

Save a workflow in the app first. Then, in the Shortcuts app, add an action and search for “Stripix”: your saved workflows appear there and in the Share Sheet. Android has no Shortcuts app: run workflows from the Workflows screen in the app, or touch and hold the Stripix icon for quick tool shortcuts.

A file won’t open or convert

Stripix decodes files on your device: with the system’s decoders on iPhone and iPad, and with the app’s built-in decoders on Android. Most RAW formats work, but support for some cameras depends on the device and system version. If a file fails, send it to us if you can, or tell us the camera and file type.

The app can’t see my photos

Check iOS Settings → Stripix → Photos. “Limited Access” works fine: the app sees only the photos you selected, and you can add more there. On Android the app needs no photo permission: it sees only what you pick with the photo picker or the Files picker.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens on your device — try it in Airplane Mode. See the Privacy Policy.
